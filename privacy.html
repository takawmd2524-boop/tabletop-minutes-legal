Tabletop Minutes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Tabletop Minutes does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK.

  • Microphone and audio: used only to record the meetings you start. Recordings are stored inside the app on your device.
  • Speech recognition: transcription runs with Apple's on-device speech recognition. The app does not send audio to us or to any server.
  • Summaries and questions: generated by the on-device Apple Intelligence language model. Transcripts are not uploaded.
  • Reminders and Calendar: accessed only when you choose to export action items to Reminders or start a meeting from a calendar event.
  • Face ID: used only to unlock the app if you turn on the lock. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Sharing: your meetings leave the device only when you export or share them yourself.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Recording other people may require their consent under the laws where you are. Please obtain consent before recording.

Contact: see the support page.

日本語
